Frequently Asked Questions

A few things you might want to know about MAISON MJ.

What is MAISON MJ? +

MAISON MJ is a fashion inspiration platform celebrating timeless style, outfit ideas and carefully selected fashion finds.

Do you sell products directly? +

MAISON MJ focuses on fashion inspiration and affiliate product recommendations. Product purchases may take place on third-party websites.

How do affiliate links work? +

Some links may be affiliate links. If you purchase through an eligible link, MAISON MJ may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Can brands collaborate with MAISON MJ? +

Yes! Brands and creators can contact us by email to discuss relevant collaborations and partnerships.

Where should I ask about an order? +

If you purchased through a third-party retailer, please contact that retailer directly for order tracking, returns, refunds and delivery support.
